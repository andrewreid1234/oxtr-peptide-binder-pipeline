Stage 0.1 — controls (new)

Positive control (oxytocin): AfCycDesign i_ptm 0.368 (mediocre), B3BPFN BBB probability 0.340 (borderline, technically a miss — oxytocin is known poorly BBB-permeable), Boltz2 iptm 0.959 (confirms its compression problem is total). Its blind unconstrained disulfide prediction was badly wrong (9.2 Å) — but the real crystallographic distance, pulled directly from 7RYC chain L (the actual solved pose, unused until now), is 2.029 Å. A structure-prediction failure for a hard no-template case, not evidence the pipeline's disulfide logic is broken.

Negative control (weakest shortlisted candidate, out_39_sample3): passed 20 ns MD indistinguishably from the "good" candidates. Real finding: MD here is a coarse pass/fail check, not a fine-grained ranking signal between candidates that already cleared Stage 4.

Disulfide-forcing check, all 27 shortlisted candidates (PyRosetta FastRelax, matched protocol): 25/27 support their designed bond better than AfCycDesign's blind prediction supported oxytocin's real one. One outlier: out_17_sample3 (forced-bond energy +2.684, worse than oxytocin) — recommend deprioritizing.
